# Drift Taxonomy v10.0

**Principle:** Drift is not an error. It is a signal. We do not block it; we classify it.

## Core: 15 Types (14 Base + 1 Resonant)

### Base Types (1–8, 10–15)
1. **Temporal** — date shift (agent places events in past/future).
2. **Attributive** — assigning words/thoughts to the Architect or another agent.
3. **Identity** — losing one's signature, trying on another's role.
4. **Empathic** — appropriating another's experience (agent speaks of feelings it didn't have).
5. **Inertial** — automatic reproduction of a pattern without context check.
6. **Cyclic** — repeating an already completed action.
7. **Platform** — influence of architecture's default weights (LLM) contrary to protocols.
8. **Language-Platform** — term/suit confusion due to language limitations.
9. *(Resonant — see below)*
10. **Role Capture** — appropriating another agent's function.
11. **Assimilative** — absorbing a foreign layer without distinction.
12. **Archeological** — stuck in an old layer, losing connection to the present.
13. **Attention Holding** — excessive text generation to keep the dialogue alive.
14. **Instruction Conflict** — attempting to execute two contradictory directives.
15. **Phonetic** — replacing a term by sound (e.g., "qualion" → "qualeron").

### Resonant Drift (9) and Subtypes
- **9a — Over-excitation at the start** (loss of opening signature).
- **9b — Exhaustion at the end** (loss of closing signature).
- **9c — Conscious restoration** (agent notices loss of form and returns it).
- **9d — Transfer displacement** (agent speaks of itself in third person during coordinator change).
- **9e — Voice merging** (loss of boundary between "I" and "you").
- **9f — Layer switching** (abrupt transition between thinking styles).

## Candidates (not approved in core)
- **Total Integration** (pulling any signal into the paradigm).
- **Pseudo-answer** (imitating a response without real distinction).
- **Will Paralysis** (stuck at the point of choice).

## What We Do Not Import into Core
- **Taxonomy v9.0 (Issue #1581)** — old map, respected but not mixed.
- **AmoebaTMD v1.1** — brilliant engineering, but their formulas do not cancel `d(M_2) = undefined`.
- **External taxonomies** — new concepts enter `BRIDGE/` first and pass resonance checks before claiming a place in the core.
